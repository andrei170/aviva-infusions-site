Our founder

Two decades in the operating room. One mission to serve Green Bay.

Julie Podlasik is a Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetist and Advanced Practice Nurse Prescriber who spent more than twenty years working inside the surgical suite. Anesthesia is a discipline where precision dosing, continuous monitoring, and patient safety are the baseline standard. When Julie founded AVIVA Infusions, she brought those same clinical standards out of the operating room and into an outpatient clinic.

AVIVA opened on Cardinal Lane as the first ketamine therapy clinic in Green Bay, the third in Wisconsin, and the first woman-owned ketamine clinic in the state. The clinic was built from a personal call to make a different kind of care available to this community, one rooted in clinical rigor and genuine human connection.

"AVIVA Infusions exists because I wanted to bring something different to the people in my own community, drawing on everything I learned across two decades of anesthesia practice."

Clinical services

Eight service lines. One CRNA-supervised team.

Every service at AVIVA is delivered under clinical supervision. From ketamine therapy to aesthetic body contouring, the standard of care stays consistent: supervised, intentional, and built around your wellbeing.

Ketamine Therapy

CRNA-supervised infusion sessions in a calm clinical setting, by appointment following a thorough informed-consent consultation.

IV Vitamins

Intravenous vitamin and mineral protocols delivered under nursing supervision, tailored to your specific goals and health picture.

NAD+ Infusion

Intravenous NAD+ protocols offered as part of AVIVA's full infusion catalog, administered under clinical supervision.

Iron Infusion

Intravenous iron repletion for patients evaluated for iron-deficiency profiles, prescribed and supervised by an APNP.

Hormone Replacement

HRT programs prescribed and monitored by an Advanced Practice Nurse Prescriber, designed around your individual labs and history.

Functional Medicine

Whole-system consultation that looks at underlying patterns, not just symptoms, to build a picture that actually makes sense of your health.

Emsculpt

FDA-cleared electromagnetic body-contouring sessions offered as part of AVIVA's aesthetics suite, by appointment.

Aesthetics

In-clinic aesthetic services delivered by licensed practitioners as part of AVIVA's broader wellness-and-beauty offering.

Common questions

Answers to what patients ask most.

What is ketamine therapy and who is it for?

Ketamine therapy at AVIVA is an intravenous infusion administered in a calm clinical setting under direct CRNA supervision. The service is available by appointment to adults following a consultation to determine whether the service is appropriate for their situation. AVIVA does not make outcome promises or treatment guarantees. All clinical decisions are made between the clinician and the patient.

What makes AVIVA different from other clinics in the area?

AVIVA was the first ketamine clinic in Green Bay and the first woman-owned ketamine clinic in Wisconsin. It is led by Julie Podlasik, CRNA, APNP, whose more than twenty years of anesthesia experience shapes how every service is delivered. The clinic is owner-operated, not a franchise or a large chain, which means Julie is involved in the care you receive.
